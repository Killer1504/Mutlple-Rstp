# 👁️ Sentinel: AI-Powered Security Dashboard

> **Turn your standard security cameras into an intelligent, proactive defense system.**

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary

**Sentinel** (MultiRtspViewer) is a next-generation security monitoring solution that brings **Artificial Intelligence** to any RTSP-enabled camera. 

Unlike traditional "dumb" viewers that just record video, Sentinel **watches** the video for you, detecting people and vehicles in real-time, 24/7. It runs entirely on your local hardware—ensuring **100% privacy** and **zero monthly cloud fees**.

---

## 🛡️ Key Features

### 1. 🤖 Local AI Verification (No Cloud)
*   **Zero Latency**: Detection happens instantly on your machine.
*   **Privacy First**: No video feeds ever leave your network. Your security data stays yours.
*   **Cost Efficient**: No expensive "per-camera" cloud subscription fees.

### 2. 🎯 Smart Detection
*   **Person Detection**: Instantly identifies unauthorized personnel or intruders.
*   **Vehicle Detection**: Monitors parking lots or driveways for vehicles.
*   **Visual Feedback**: Draws clear red indicators around detected objects for immediate operator awareness.

### 3. 🖥️ "Cyberpunk" Command Center
*   **Immersive UI**: Designed for high-performance security operations centers (SOC).
*   **Multi-View Grid**: Monitor 1, 4, 9, or 16 cameras simultaneously.
*   **Status at a Glance**: Instant visual indicators for offline cameras or network issues.

### 4. ⚡ Resilience & Reliability
*   **Auto-Healing**: If a camera connection drops, the system automatically attempts to reconnect using smart algorithms.
*   **Hardware Accelerated**: optimized to use your GPU for smooth 4K playback across multiple streams.
